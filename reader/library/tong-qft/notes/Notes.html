Notes

Explanations of things that didn’t stick on a first reading of Tong. Each one is linked from the sentence in the lectures where the question came up: the phrase is highlighted there, and it opens this page.

So far

  • The Heisenberg inequality. Why a particle trapped in a box of size $L$ has $\Delta p \geq \hbar/L$. Asked at the first mention in the introduction.
  • The Compton wavelength. Where $\hbar/(mc)$ comes from, and why a smaller box is full of pairs. Asked at the pair sentence.
  • The de Broglie wavelength. Why $\lambda_{dB} = h/|\vec p|$, and the sense in which it is longer than the Compton length. Asked at the first mention.
  • Relativistic one-particle wave equations. Negative probabilities, the negative-energy tower, and the loss of causality. Asked at the parenthesis in the introduction.
  • Spin and statistics. What is experimental in quantum mechanics, and what relativity forces. Asked at the statistics sentence.
  • Hilbert space and operator-valued fields. What the space is, and what it means to promote a classical coordinate, or a whole field, to an operator. Asked at the quantization sentence.
  • The Planck scale. The 1899 units, and the length, time, mass, and energy they fix. Asked at the Planck scale.
  • Pointlike electrons, extended protons. What scattering measures, and why spin is not the distinction. Asked at the word “pointlike”.
