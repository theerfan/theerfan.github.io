Papers cited in the lectures, kept as local copies, and companions that work through them.

Local copies

  • Frank Wilczek, “Quantum Field Theory” (1998). A review of the principles and successes of quantum field theory. Cited in the introduction.

Companions

  • Dirac 1931: a companion. A section-by-section guide to “Quantised Singularities in the Electromagnetic Field”: what a non-integrable phase is, why it acts as an electromagnetic potential, and how the $2\pi$ ambiguity of a phase leads to magnetic poles and the quantization of charge. Cited in chapter 1.
